# Sahel Agro Intelligence

### Geospatial Intelligence for Agriculture, Water, and Climate Resilience in the Sahel

Sahel Agro Intelligence is an agricultural intelligence platform being developed to combine **satellite imagery, weather, soil, geospatial data, and machine learning** into practical intelligence for agriculture across Chad and the wider Sahel.

The project is designed to help transform fragmented environmental and agricultural data into understandable signals for **farm monitoring, crop health, drought analysis, water intelligence, flood risk, soil assessment, crop suitability, and agricultural planning**.

> **Project status:** Early development  
> **Geographic focus:** Chad → Sahel → Africa  
> **Foundation:** OpenFarm  
> **License:** BSD-3-Clause

---

## 🌍 The Problem

Agriculture across the Sahel operates under difficult and highly variable environmental conditions.

Farmers and agricultural organizations may need to make decisions while dealing with:

- irregular rainfall
- drought
- flooding
- water scarcity
- soil degradation
- extreme temperatures
- changing growing conditions
- fragmented agricultural information
- limited access to geospatial intelligence

At the same time, enormous amounts of useful information already exist in satellite imagery, weather observations, soil databases, and geographic datasets.

The challenge is turning those observations into **usable intelligence**.

---

# 🎯 Our Mission

Build an open, transparent, and technically rigorous intelligence platform that helps answer questions such as:

> **What is happening in this field?**

> **Where is crop stress occurring?**

> **How are weather and soil conditions affecting agricultural conditions?**

> **Where are drought or water-related risks increasing?**

> **Which areas may be suitable for particular crops?**

> **How are conditions changing over time?**

The platform is being developed with a strong focus on the realities of **Chad and the Sahel**.
